Get to know us

Kats Nails provides professional nail care, nail art and beauty services for all occasions

This website is created for my nail business to showcase services, attract customers, and provide important information. The main objective of the website is to promote the business online and make it easy for customers to view services, prices, and contact details. This business started mainly because of my mother, she had a nail business, and I kept admiring her work and I just learned just by looking at her work. That inspired me to start my own nail business at young age of 17, and it’s been booming ever since. It’s been 2 wonderful years since this business was launched.

The target audience includes mainly young adults (18-30) and women (35-50) as well as men who need to be pampered from time to time, young learners who attend prom, or anyone who are interested in nail care and beauty services for a special occasion. These users are familiar with smartphones and social media, so they expect a modern, fast, and visually appealing website.
