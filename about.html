About Kats Nails

Kats Nails provides professional nail care, nail art, and beauty services for every occasion.

This business was inspired by a passion for beauty, creativity, and self-care. What started as admiration for my mother’s nail business grew into my own successful salon journey at the age of 17.

For the past two years, Kats Nails has continued to grow, helping clients feel confident, stylish, and pampered with high-quality nail services and modern nail art.

Our goal is to provide a relaxing experience while delivering elegant nail designs that match every personality and occasion.
